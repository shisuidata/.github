14:## We build for data work that must hold up under scrutiny.
15:
16:**Shisui** (拾穗) means gathering what others overlook. We bring that idea to the space between AI agents and real data systems: preserve context, expose evidence, constrain execution, and keep people in control.
17:
18:No magic black boxes. No confusing generated output with trusted results. Just open tools and practical methods for making data work more reviewable, reproducible, and useful.

51:## Our working principles
52:
53:| | Principle | What it means |
54:|:--|:--|:--|
55:| `01` | **Constrain before execution** | An AI-generated candidate is input to a process—not permission to act. |
56:| `02` | **Evidence over confidence** | Important outputs should be inspectable, attributable, and reproducible. |
57:| `03` | **Humans stay in control** | High-impact actions need clear boundaries and deliberate approval. |
58:| `04` | **Methods must survive real work** | Useful systems handle ambiguity, permissions, failure, and handoff—not only demos. |
